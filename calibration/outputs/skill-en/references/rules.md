# Figure rules

This document holds the rules figure-check applies to every figure and introduces the rules for each figure type and for special cases.
Read the general rules for every manuscript, and read the documents for a type only when the manuscript has figures of that type.

## General rules

Every figure follows these rules:

- **Font size**: Text in figures is at least 6 pt at final print size.
- **Fonts**: Fonts are consistent across figures.
- **Panel labels**: Panels are labeled (a, b, c) consistently, and the caption refers to each panel.
- **Color**: Color schemes stay readable for color-blind readers and never rely on red-green contrast alone.
  - When the journal prints in grayscale, the figure must also work in grayscale.
- **Resolution**: Raster images are at least 300 dpi at print size.

A journal's guidelines can replace these values, as [journal guidelines](journals.md) describes.

## Rules by figure type

The rules for figure types are in these documents.
A figure that combines panel types gets each type's rules per panel, as [multi-panel figures](rules/multi-panel.md) explains.
The documents are:

- [Multi-panel figures](rules/multi-panel.md): grid alignment, shared axis ranges, and consistent units across panels.
- [Plots](rules/plots.md): axes, legends, error bars, significance markers, chart types, color maps, and sample sizes.
- [Micrographs](rules/micrographs.md): scale bars, image adjustments, pseudo-color, and insets.
- [Photographs and diagrams](rules/photographs-and-diagrams.md): scale references and consent for photographs, and legends and consistency for diagrams.

## Special cases

[Special cases](rules/special-cases.md) covers gel and blot images, figures reused from earlier publications, and supplementary figures.
Read it when the manuscript has any of these.
