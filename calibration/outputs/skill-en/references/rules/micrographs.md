# Rules for micrographs

This document lists the rules figure-check applies to micrographs, in addition to the [general rules](../rules.md#general-rules).
They concern scale bars, image adjustments, pseudo-color, and insets.

The rules are:

- **Scale bar**: Micrographs have scale bars with a stated length.
  - A magnification value does not replace a scale bar.
- **Adjustments**: Contrast and brightness adjustments are applied to the whole image and disclosed in the methods or caption.
  - When only the final image is available, the skill cannot establish whether an adjustment was uniform and flags the figure as "needs author check", as [limitations](../limitations.md) describes.
- **Pseudo-color**: Pseudo-color is described in the caption.
- **Insets**: Insets are marked on the parent image with a box.
