# Rules for multi-panel figures

This document lists the rules figure-check applies to figures made of several panels.
It applies in addition to the [general rules](../rules.md#general-rules), and each panel is also checked against the rules for its own type.

## Panel layout

These rules govern how panels relate to each other:

- **Grid**: Panels are aligned on a common grid.
- **Axis ranges**: Panels that are meant to be compared share axis ranges, or the caption says that they do not.
- **Units**: Units are consistent across panels.

## Mixed panel types

A figure that combines panel types, such as a micrograph next to a plot, gets each type's rules per panel.
The [rules](../rules.md#rules-by-figure-type) document lists the documents for each type.
