# Rules for photographs and diagrams

This document lists the rules figure-check applies to photographs other than micrographs and to diagrams, in addition to the [general rules](../rules.md#general-rules).
Micrographs have their own [rules](micrographs.md).
Both kinds of figure are short and are checked by the same reader in the same pass, so they share this document.

## Photographs

Photographs such as field or specimen photos follow these rules:

- **Scale reference**: A photograph includes a scale reference when size matters.
- **Consent**: A photograph showing an identifiable person needs a consent statement in the caption or methods.
  - A missing statement is a must-fix issue.

## Diagrams

Diagrams follow these rules:

- **Symbols**: Arrows and symbols are explained in a legend or the caption.
- **Consistency**: Line styles and shapes are used consistently within the diagram.
