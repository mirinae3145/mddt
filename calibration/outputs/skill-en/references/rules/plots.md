# Rules for plots

This document lists the rules figure-check applies to plots, in addition to the [general rules](../rules.md#general-rules).
The rules concern axes, annotations and the caption, and chart types and color.

## Axes

These rules concern the axes:

- **Labels**: Axes carry labels with units, and logarithmic scales are marked as such.
- **Truncation**: Axes are not truncated without break marks.
- **Bar charts**: Bar charts start at zero.

## Legends, error bars, and significance

These rules concern what a reader needs to interpret the data:

- **Legends**: Legends do not cover data.
- **Error bars**: Error bars are defined in the caption (SD or SEM, and n).
- **Significance markers**: Markers such as asterisks are defined with their p thresholds.
- **Sample size**: The caption states n for each group shown.

## Chart types and color maps

These rules concern the choice of chart and color map:

- **Color maps**: Continuous data uses a perceptually uniform color map.
  - Rainbow maps such as jet are a should-fix issue.
- **Pie charts**: Pie charts with more than five slices get a suggestion to use a bar chart.
