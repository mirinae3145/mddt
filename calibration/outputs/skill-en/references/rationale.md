# Rationale

This document explains why figure-check reviews figures the way it does.
It gives the reasons behind the rules for clarity, color, and image integrity.

## Why the rules exist

Three reasons shape the rules:

- **Clarity**: Reviewers reject unclear figures, so rules on font size, labels, units, and captions aim to make each figure readable without guessing.
- **Color vision**: Color-blind readers are about one in twelve men, so the [general rules](rules.md#general-rules) forbid relying on red-green contrast alone.
- **Integrity**: Journals and institutions have integrity rules on image manipulation, so the rules on adjustments and [gel and blot images](rules/special-cases.md) follow from them.
